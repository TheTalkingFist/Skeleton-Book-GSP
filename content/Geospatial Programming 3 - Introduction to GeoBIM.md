# BIM(Building Info Modelling)

BIM, a process involving the generation and management of digital representation of the physical and functional characteristics of places.

Supported by various tools, techs and contracts with which its files can be extracted, exchanged & Networked to support decision-making regarding a built asset.

The software is used by individuals, businesses and government agencies who plan, design, construct, operate and maintain buildings and diverse physical infrastructure, such as water, refuse, electricity, gas, communication utilities, roads, railways, bridges, ports and tunnels.

## B (BUILDING)
"to build"

	BIM is a process involving building something together, be it relating to architecture, infrastructure, civil engineering, landscaping or large-scale projects.

## I (INFORMATION)
"embedded information"

	Contails details of various aspects of the building project such as components, identities, material types, fire rating, warranties, prices, etc that  make the BIM model "smart".

## M (MODELLING)
"digital representation"

	Creating a virtual model of the building project - for visualising design , for managing construction process, for operating and maintaining the building etc.

**Embedded Information = Geometric + Non-Geometric**


# Intersections between GSP and BIM at different scales

## Design + Build

- Deals with the very specific
- Detailed drawings & information of buildings 
- Rich information of building materials & physical elements of an individual building
## Operate + Plan

- Deal with the Universal(broad)
- Generic details about spatial phenomena, features, objects
- Information can span across wide region

## GeoBIM Case Studies

Visualize existing and potential design impact on the environment and landscape

Create realistic digital twins that are according to scale and geo-reference:  
- Accurately tagged fixed and moving assets in the virtual world in association to their physical presence.  
- Live-data from sensors and building systems to improve planning and facility operational efficiency

# Benefits of integrating GeoBIM Integration and Digital Twins

1. Increase accuracy of 3D models for accurate tracking of fixed and moving assets
2. Provide accurate building and spatial information for tenant management, events planning and facility management 
3. Provide simulation capabilities for space optimization, route planning and crowd management

Digital twins are virtual representations or model of a physical object, system or process.
