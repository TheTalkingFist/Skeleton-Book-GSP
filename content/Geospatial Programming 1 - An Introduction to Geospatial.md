
# What is GSP?

GSP (Geospatial) collects relatively large data to geotag and visualize the data assets. This enables  fine grained analysis and predictions which also supports data-driven policy making.

Examples of how GSP helps Includes: Urban Planning, Social Service Planning, Environment (Transboundary Haze), Movement of Ppl and Goods.


## Geospatial Technology

- Technology of GSP Includes:
	-  Mapping and Surveying techniques like;
		  -  GPS (Global Positioning System), GIS (Geographic Info systems)
- Remote Sensing
- Cartography
- Spatial Analysis
- Location-Based Services

Remember GGRCSL. Geylang GRC SL.


## Use Cases

- 3D City planning and BIM modelling
- Indoor Navigating and barrier free access
- AR/VR for collaborative decision making
- Autonomous Vehicles and 3D routing
- Spatial-temporal geoanalytics
- Predictive analytics through machine learning
- Big(Geo)Data Internet of things
- Open-Source Technologies

# What are the Benefits?

- Simplifies Complex data to help in decision making
- Maintains transparency in data for citizen access
- Improved Communication during a crisis for better crisis management
- Help in managing natural resources
- Help government in making better decisions
- Help to discover precautions  before planning development changes in a community
